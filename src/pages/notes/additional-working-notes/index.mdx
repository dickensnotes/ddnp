## Expanding Dickens Notes

_Dickens Notes_ is working to transcribe and annotate all of Charles Dickens's working notes. In 2025, we received a two-year National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH) Scholarly Editions grant to complete the project and incorporate manuscript images of the working notes into our Mirador platform for all editions (grant period: January 2026 to December 2027).

By December 2027, _Dickens Notes_ will feature complete editions of all Dickens's working notes: 

**_The Old Curiosity Shop_ (1840-41)**  
Early planning documents that show us the first stages of Dickens's notetaking practice as he wrote this weekly novel for _Master Humphrey's Clock_.  
Edition due for release June 2027.  
Manuscript images provided by the V&A National Art Library.  
Primary Editor: Anna Gibson

**_Martin Chuzzlewit_ (1843-44)**  
Early planning documents that show us the first stages of Dickens’s working note system.  
Edition due for release June 2027.  
Manuscript images provided by the V&A National Art Library.  
Primary editor: Adam Grener 

**_Dombey and Son_ (1846-48)**  
Dickens’s first full set of systematic working notes.  
Edition due for release December 2026.  
Manuscript images provided by the V&A National Art Library.  
Primary editor: Adam Grener 

**_David Copperfield_ (1849-50)**  
Working notes for Dickens’s most autobiographical novel.  
Edition published in 2023 (V1.1).  
Manuscript images to be added 2026 courtesy of the V&A National Art Library.  
Primary editor: Frankie Goodenough 

**_Bleak House_ (1852-53)**  
Notes that show Dickens’s complex management of dual narrators.  
Edition published in 2022 (V1).  
Manuscript images to be added 2026 courtesy of the V&A National Art Library.  
Primary editor: Adam Grener 

**_Hard Times_ (1854)**  
The only complete set of working notes for a weekly novel.  
Edition published in 2023 (V1.3).  
Manuscript images to be added 2026 courtesy of the V&A National Art Library.  
Primary editor: Adam Grener

**_Little Dorrit_ (1855-57)**  
Comprehensive set of working notes that include two pages of “Mems: for working the story round” before the notes for the final double number.  
Edition published in 2023 (V1.3).  
Manuscript images to be added 2026 courtesy of the V&A National Art Library.  
Primary editor: Anna Gibson

**_Great Expectations_ (1860-61)**  
Three pages of planning documents for this weekly novel.  
Edition due for release December 2027.  
Manuscript images provided by the Wisbech and Fenland Museum, Cambridgeshire.  
Primary Editor: Anna Gibson and Adam Grener

**_Our Mutual Friend_ (1864-65)**  
Full set of working notes for Dickens’s last completed monthly novel.  
Edition due for release December 2027.  
Manuscript images provided by the Morgan Library, New York.  
Primary Editor: Anna Gibson

**_The Mystery of Edwin Drood_ (1870)**  
Partial set of working notes for I-VI, and Dickens’s blank headed sheets for the prospective numbers VII-XII. (Dickens died while he was writing this novel.)  
Edition due for release December 2026.  
Manuscript images provided by the V&A National Art Library.  
Primary editor: Anna Gibson

## Integrating Manuscript Images

Completing the _Dickens Notes_ editions of Dickens’s working notes includes integrating images of the manuscripts alongside our visually faithful transcriptions in Mirador. Users will be able to toggle between manuscript image and transcription image to view Dickens’s hand and decipher his handwriting. We are grateful to the NEH for supporting this work, and to the libraries that hold these manuscripts for their support and collaboration: the Victoria & Albert Museum’s National Art Library in London; the Morgan Library in New York; and the Wisbech and Fenland Museum in Cambridgeshire.
